# Advanced settings

Use the shared `ds-advanced-dialog` class for advanced settings dialogs. Keep the dialog compact (up to 540 px), arrange short controls in two columns where space permits, and let descriptions or multiline inputs use a full row. At narrow widths use one column. Input padding remains governed by the design-system field tokens and must not exceed 48 px per side. Keep option labels readable and avoid truncating user-entered values.
